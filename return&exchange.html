Return & Exchange Policy

At WatchVerse, we prioritize your satisfaction. If you’re not completely happy with your purchase, we offer a straightforward return and exchange process. Please read the following policy carefully before initiating a return or exchange.

Eligibility for Returns

  • Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
  • The product must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags and accessories intact.
  • Items that show signs of wear or damage will not be eligible for return.

Exchange Policy

If you received a defective or incorrect item, we will gladly exchange it for the correct one at no additional cost. Exchanges are also allowed for size or model changes, subject to stock availability.

How to Initiate a Return

To start your return or exchange, please email us at support@watchverse.com with your order number and photos of the product.

Once your return is approved and received, the refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

For more help, reach out to us anytime at support@watchverse.com. We’re here to assist you!
